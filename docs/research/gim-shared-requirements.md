# Group Ironman Shared Item Requirements Research (`RUNELITE-480`)

> **Scope:** Cited reference of Old School RuneScape items where **obtaining** requires a quest, achievement diary, minigame, boss-area unlock, or high skill level, while **equipping or using** does not — allowing one Group Ironman (GIM) member to unlock and supply the item to teammates via direct trade or Group Storage. Also documents common "false positive" items whose **equip/use** check still enforces a quest, boss-KC, miniquest, or skill check, and **untradeable** items that cannot be shared at all.
>
> **Companion data file:** [`src/main/resources/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/gim-shared-requirements.json`](../../src/main/resources/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/gim-shared-requirements.json) (not wired into plugin code in this task).

## 0. Group Ironman Trading, Storage & Prestige Rules

Before checking individual items, three game-wide rules govern whether a groupmate can pass and use an item:

1. **Standard tradeability governs Group Storage and GIM trades (`Tradeable: Yes` only).**
   Group Storage rejects untradeable items (`"You can't deposit untradeable items"` — [Transcript: Group Storage tutor](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Transcript:Group_Storage_tutor)). Jagex surveyed "tradeable untradeables" between GIM teammates in early 2022 ([Update: Group Ironman: Post Launch Improvements](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Update:Group_Ironman:_Post_Launch_Improvements)) and **decided against shipping it** due to quest-check, charge-display, and maintenance issues ([Update: Unranked Group Ironman Mode](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Update:Unranked_Group_Ironman_Mode)). Degradable and charged gear must therefore be in its **tradeable state** (`100%`/new or `0%`/broken for Barrows and Perilous Moons armour; uncharged/empty for powered staves, Toxic blowpipe, Serpentine helm, Venator bow, Pharaoh's sceptre, and elemental tomes; disassembled for Slayer helmet and Chugging barrel) before depositing or trading ([Group Ironman Mode](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Group_Ironman_Mode)).
2. **Original-roster Ranked GIM and all Unranked GIM members have zero GIM-specific boss-KC equip gates.**
   If the entire group started together as fresh accounts on The Node, every member retains **Individual Prestige** and has **no** GIM anti-boosting kill-count requirements or wealth caps when receiving items from teammates ([Update: Group Ironman](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Update:Group_Ironman), [Group Ironman Mode](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Group_Ironman_Mode)). Game-wide item equip checks (such as the 1-kill boss requirement on Desert Treasure II ancient rings; see §1A and §2) still apply to all accounts.
3. **Newly added Ranked GIM members (no Individual Prestige) face temporary anti-boosting caps.**
   A player who joins an existing Ranked GIM group after creation lacks Individual Prestige: for their first 5 weeks they have weekly wealth transfer caps (`1M` → `20M` → `50M` → `100M` → unlimited in Week 5+) and **cannot equip content-restricted boss/Slayer/raid uniques** (such as Abyssal whip, Trident of the seas, Cerberus boots, Ferocious gloves, Dragon hunter lance, Brimstone ring, CoX/ToB uniques) until they get **1 kill count** on the corresponding boss/monster (or until restrictions lift after Week 5; Unranked GIM has no restrictions at all) ([Group Ironman Mode](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Group_Ironman_Mode), [Talk: Group Ironman Mode](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Talk:Group_Ironman_Mode)).

Every item below is **tradeable within the group** (in the noted state where applicable) and can be equipped or consumed **without** the full quest, diary, minigame, or gathering/production skill required to obtain it. Partial bypasses where a lower or mid-quest requirement still applies (e.g. *DT2 rings*, *Boots of Brimstone*, *Pharaoh's sceptre*) are noted explicitly in the **Requirement to Equip / Use** column.

## 4. Explicitly Unverified Edge Cases (No Guessing)

Per project research rules, claims where OSRS Wiki pages conflict or omit Ironman-specific shop behaviour are flagged as **unverified** rather than guessed, and are **excluded from `shareableWithoutObtainReq: true` hints** in `gim-shared-requirements.json` until tested in-game:

1. **`Guthix rest(4)` (`4417`) — Drinking without *One Small Favour* [UNVERIFIED]:**
   - [Guthix rest](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Guthix_rest) states *"If players attempt to make Guthix rest before starting One Small Favour, they will receive a message in their chatbox stating, 'You have no idea what effect this would have'"* and [Making Guthix rests](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Money_making_guide/Making_Guthix_rests) notes widespread PvP usage, **whereas** [One Small Favour/Quick guide](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/One_Small_Favour/Quick_guide) lists *"The ability to make and drink Guthix rest tea"* under quest rewards and `Guthix rest`'s narrative text says *"Once Sanfew explains how players can make it during the One Small Favour quest, they unlock the ability to consume and make their own."* Because the wiki contradicts itself on whether *drinking* (as opposed to *making*) requires reaching Sanfew in *One Small Favour*, this is marked **unverified**.
2. **`Rune pouch note` (`24587`) — Purchasing on a Group Ironman at Justine's LMS shop [UNVERIFIED]:**
   - [Rune pouch note](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Rune_pouch_note) is a GE-tradeable promissory note sold at [Justine's stuff for the Last Shopper Standing](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Justine%27s_stuff_for_the_Last_Shopper_Standing) for `75` LMS points (requiring 1 LMS win to buy tradeable items per [Update: Gathering QoL, Trouver Changes & more!](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Update:Gathering_QoL,_Trouver_Changes_%26_more!)). However, the wiki articles read here do not explicitly state whether Group Ironmen are permitted to purchase the tradeable `Rune pouch note` (and pass it to a groupmate) or are restricted to untradeable Ironman reward variants. Marked **unverified**.

## 5. Code Map for Future GIM Shopping Integration (Not Wired in This Task)

When a future task wires [`gim-shared-requirements.json`](../../src/main/resources/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/gim-shared-requirements.json) into GIM Utilities' Shopping / Request / Compare panels:

- **Bundled resource path:** `src/main/resources/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/gim-shared-requirements.json` (same package directory as [`quest-items.json`](../../src/main/resources/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/quest-items.json), [`shopping-presets.json`](../../src/main/resources/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/shopping-presets.json), and [`item-requirements.txt`](../../src/main/resources/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/item-requirements.txt)).
- **Existing reachability & requirement readers:**
  - [`ShopSources.java:99-140`](../../src/main/java/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/ShopSources.java#L99-L140) already judges whether the local `Viewer` (`levels` + `QuestProgress`) is blocked from a shop (`unreachableShops`).
  - [`MemberProgressBook.java`](../../src/main/java/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/MemberProgressBook.java) and [`AchievementState.java`](../../src/main/java/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/AchievementState.java) already hold synced groupmates' skill levels, completed quests, and diary tiers.
  - [`ItemRequirements.java:16-67`](../../src/main/java/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/ItemRequirements.java#L16-L67) and [`BenefitFinder.java:118-140`](../../src/main/java/com/vinnyvangogh/groupironman/BenefitFinder.java#L118-L140) evaluate skill requirements for *"Who benefits most"* in Compare.
- **How `gim-shared-requirements.json` supports both positive hints and trap warnings:**
  - Entries with `"shareableWithoutObtainReq": true` power *"A groupmate can get this for you (`obtainReq`); you only need `equipReq` to use it"* (and can even name which synced teammate already meets `obtainReq`).
  - Entries with `"shareableWithoutObtainReq": false` and `"tradeable": true` prevent false hints on items like *Dragon scimitar*, *DT2 ancient rings* (without 1 boss kill), *Helm of neitiznot*, *Zamorakian hasta*, or *Anti-dragon shield* (*"Requires `equipReq` to equip even if traded"*).
  - Entries with `"tradeable": false` surface *"Untradeable — each group member must earn their own"* (or note component workarounds like trading *Blood shard* + *Amulet of fury* before combining).
