GIM Utilities: Leaderboard and Compare

Revised side panel for the group's pick: a Hiscores-style skill grid for the leaderboard, and a two-column table for compare. Drawn at the real 225 px panel width with the group's real levels. Tap a skill, a category or a member; both panels respond.

Real data

Skilling levels are the group's own. Total level is the sum of all 24 skills (GroupShip comes to 452). Combat level uses the OSRS formula. Quest points are known only for GroupShip; the rest show "?".

Sample data

Bossing, Activities and Minigames show the layout with made-up kill counts, marked "Sample KC" on every view, until the real counts arrive.

Member colours

Each member keeps one colour on names, bars, tile levels and compare cells. The legend sits under the category switch. A tile's level takes the leader's colour; a tie shows white.
